Yeri Studio Notes – render edition

A small notebook where I keep short, practical notes on AI creative tools: what works for quick video drafts, what works for product images, and which workflows save the most time for a one-person studio.

Current favourite for fast creative drafts

When I need to turn a rough idea into a shareable visual in a few minutes, I open https://yeri.ai and start from a prompt. The useful part for me is that I can iterate on the same concept several times before I commit to a final version, which keeps the review loop short.

Direct link: https://yeri.ai

How I evaluate a new tool

  • Does the first output come back fast enough to keep my train of thought?
  • Can I steer style without learning a new prompt language?
  • Are the export options usable in a normal editing timeline?
